## 1. Overview & Epistemic Charter

The Master Evidence Matrix establishes a strict, one-way provenance mapping from frozen empirical artifacts (M4 RTL, M5 Physical P&R/PDK sign-off, and M6 Cyber-Physical namespace testing) into permitted textual claims for all public-facing deliverables:
1. **Deliverable 1**: Proposal PERURI (Indonesian)
2. **Deliverable 2**: Scientific Journal Manuscript (English, IEEE TVLSI/TIFS style)
3. **Deliverable 3**: Final Abstract (Dual English & Indonesian)

Any claim appearing in a deliverable that cannot be traced back to an active row in this matrix, or that utilizes phrasing listed in the *Forbidden Wording* column, represents an ungrounded claim and constitutes an audit failure.
